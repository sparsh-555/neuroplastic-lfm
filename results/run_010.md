## Analysis

### Verdict: MLP ≥ CfC (ΔAP = -0.020, MLP wins)

The MLP outperforms CfC by 2.0 AP points overall. The gap has two drivers:

**1. MultiRC (MLP +0.100):**  
This is the same convergence failure seen in runs 008/009. CfC loss at step 500 = ~0.20 (not converged). MLP converges on multirc in 500 steps (simpler optimizer landscape, no recurrent state to stabilize). This single task gap accounts for almost the entire AP difference.

**2. DBpedia (MLP +0.020):**  
Small gap; both converge to near-zero loss on this 14-class task.

**CfC wins yelp (+0.010) and imdb (+0.010):**  
Small but consistent wins. The token-level τ dynamics may help on sentiment tasks where long-range context is important. Marginal.

### What this means for the paper

**CfC's ODE dynamics do not provide a clear accuracy advantage over a plain 2-layer MLP at 187K params / 500 steps.**

Two interpretations:
- **Capacity/steps interpretation:** CfC's recurrence introduces a harder optimization landscape. At 500 steps with 200 training examples, the MLP reaches lower loss faster (4× per-step speed means same-compute MLP trains ~8× as long). CfC may need more steps to manifest its advantage.
- **Architecture interpretation:** The τ dynamics are genuinely unnecessary for this benchmark — a simple projection is sufficient.

At this training scale (200 examples, 500 steps), the simpler model wins. This is the honest result.

### Paper framing implication

Per the pre-defined criteria (set before seeing results):
- CfC > MLP by >1 AP → CfC substrate justified as core contribution
- CfC ≈ MLP (within 1 AP) → NCP-wired adapter framing, CfC is one option
- MLP > CfC → same as above

**Decision: pivot to "NCP-wired growing adapter" framing.**

The core contribution is the growing-architecture CL method:
1. Zero forgetting by construction (PNN-style, immutable prior clusters)
2. Label-free at inference (no task routing required)
3. ~2× fewer params than per-task LoRA per task
4. Model-agnostic injection via forward hook

CfC is presented as the default substrate with a note that simpler adapters (MLP) also work and train faster. The NCP sparse wiring remains interesting as a design property (interpretable connectivity) but is not the primary differentiator.

**The CL properties (BWT=0, F.Ra=0) are unaffected by this result** — both CfC and MLP have identical forgetting guarantees.
