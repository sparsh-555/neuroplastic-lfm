## What Happened

### The gate opened too far

The 20× maturity lr was too aggressive. Over 2000 steps the `maturity` parameter climbed from -3.0 to approximately +5.5. `sigmoid(5.5) ≈ 0.996`. By step 500 the gate was already at 0.72; by step 1000 it was at 0.93; by the end it was at 0.986.

At gate≈1.0, the cluster is no longer *correcting* the base model — it is *replacing* it. The model's output becomes:

```
h_effective = h_base + 0.986 × cluster_output
```

If `cluster_output` has comparable magnitude to `h_base`, this destroys the carefully calibrated representations that `lm_head` expects. The base model spent 1.17B parameters learning where to point hidden states; the cluster overwhelms that signal with its own learned vector.

### Catastrophic overfitting from too many cycles

150 training examples × 2000 steps ÷ 38 batches/epoch = **53 passes through the same data**. Training loss dropped to 0.20 (near zero), but eval PPL climbed from 3.15 to 5.14. The cluster memorised the 150 training examples perfectly. On the 50 unseen eval examples, it pushes hidden states in completely wrong directions.

Note: even LoRA r=8 would overfit in 53 cycles on 150 examples. The problem is not unique to CfC.

### TIES merge amplified the damage

Both clusters are deeply overfit to different distributions. TIES-merging two overfit clusters does not cancel their damage — it produces a merged cluster that is worse than either individual one (5.69 > 5.14, 7.82 > 7.10). This is the correct TIES behavior when inputs are noise: it preserves the consistent (but wrong) signal and averages the divergent parts.

### τ_a barely shifted despite full gate opening

Science cluster τ_a: `0.0008 → 0.0037`. Gate went from 0.047 to 0.986 — the maturity parameter absorbed most of the optimisation pressure. With gate at near-1.0, the path-of-least-resistance for minimising loss is to push `maturity` upward (one scalar, large gradient) rather than to tune the CfC time constants (thousands of parameters, smaller individual gradients). The τ dynamics are not being exercised.

## Root Cause Summary

| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| Maturity lr ×20 too aggressive | Gate opens to 0.99 within 500 steps, cluster replaces base model |
| 150 examples × 53 cycles | Near-zero training loss, severe overfitting, eval PPL collapses |
| Maturity scalar absorbs all gradient | CfC time constants (τ) barely trained, novelty signal absent |
| Gate unbounded — no ceiling | `maturity` can grow indefinitely; `sigmoid` asymptotes to 1.0 |

## Fixes Applied (committed for Run 005)

**1. Reduce maturity lr multiplier: 20× → 5×**
Enough to break the bootstrapping trap; not enough to blow the gate open in the first 500 steps.

**2. Hard gate ceiling: `cluster.maturity.data.clamp_(max=0.0)` after every optimizer step**
`sigmoid(0) = 0.5` — hard limit of 50% cluster influence. Prevents the cluster from replacing the base model regardless of how many steps are run. The gate can approach 0.5 asymptotically but never exceed it.

**3. MAX_STEPS: 2000 → 1000**
Reduces training cycles.

**4. TRAIN_SIZE: 150 → 400**
400 examples, batch_size=4 → ~100 batches/epoch. 1000 steps = 10 cycles through data. Compared to run 4's 53 cycles on 150 examples, this is a fundamentally different (and healthier) training regime.
